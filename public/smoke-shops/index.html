FAQ

Smoke shop questions.

Can you shoot with our neon and lights on?

Yes. We shoot with your lights, neon and display cases on, so your shop looks on Google the way it feels in person.

Is this considered advertising?

No. This is a tour and photos of your shop on your own Google Business Profile, not a paid ad, so it isn't subject to the ad restrictions that block tobacco and vape promotions.

Will customers be in the shots?

We schedule the shoot for a quiet time, usually right at opening, so customers stay out of the shots.

Which package fits a smoke shop?

Most single-room shops fit Google Visibility ($395). Shops with a glass room, lounge or several rooms usually choose Google Visibility+ ($595).

When do I pay?

At the shoot. Booking a time is free and there is no deposit.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Your tour is published to Google and can be embedded on your website with no hosting fees.
