FAQ

Straight answers.

When do I pay?

At the shoot. Booking a time is free and there is no deposit.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Your tour is published to Google and can be embedded on your website with no monthly hosting fee. Only custom interactive experiences are quoted separately.

Which package do I need?

It depends on the size of your space. Google Visibility covers up to 10 connected 360° photospheres, Google Visibility+ up to 20 and Total Visibility up to 30. If you're not sure, I'll recommend one when I see your space.

What do I actually get?

A connected 360° walkthrough of your business published to Google Maps, your Google Business Profile and Street View, plus 10, 20 or 30 professionally edited point-of-interest photos, depending on your package. You also get embed code to put the tour on your website.

Can I put the tour on my own website?

Yes. Every package includes the ability to embed your Google 360 tour on your website.

Who owns the photos?

Every package includes commercial usage rights, so you can use your photos on your website, social media and ads.

Do I need to close during the shoot?

Usually not. Many businesses book before opening or after closing so customers stay out of the shots. Pick the time that works for you.

What areas do you serve?

Based in Central Florida and serving businesses throughout Central and South Florida, from the Gulf Coast to the Atlantic Coast and across the I-4 corridor.
